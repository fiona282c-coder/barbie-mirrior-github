The idea

A little magic in the mirror.

Choosing an outfit should feel playful. Our smart mirror concept helps you explore clothes on a live image of yourself, compare looks, and find the pieces you love.

A camera above the mirror captures your full body. A computer built into the mirror processes the image and shows your selected outfit on the display. Your real clothes stay the same.

Behind the mirror

How it works

Three simple steps turn a camera view into a virtual outfit preview.

01

Capture

The camera above the mirror captures a live, full-body image of the user.

02

Track

The built-in computer processes the image and locates key body positions, including the shoulders and waist.

03

Preview

The selected outfit appears on the mirror display. The user’s real clothes stay unchanged.
